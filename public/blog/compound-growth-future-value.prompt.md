# Kitfolio Blog Cover Image Prompt

## Series identity

- **Brand:** Kitfolio
- **Use:** Editorial blog cover
- **Format:** 16:9, 1200×675
- **Visual language:** Clean SaaS, semi-flat vector infographic
- **Background:** Very light blue-gray, primarily `#F2F5FF`
- **Primary colors:** `#2D5DC8`, `#6486EF`
- **Supporting colors:** `#D4D9E5`, `#61646F`
- **Optional highlight:** A tiny amount of muted amber, under 5% of the image
- **Composition:** Low-to-medium density, centered visual balance, 15-20% outer negative space
- **Shape language:** Soft rounded rectangles, circles, thin connector lines, subtle nodes
- **Rendering:** Flat or semi-flat, front-facing orthographic view, minimal soft shadow
- **Consistency rule:** Use the same palette, line weight, corner treatment, background tone, and object density across the full nine-image series

## Article

**복리의 힘 | 복리 성장과 미래값 예측 이해하기**

## Core visual idea

복리는 단순 상승이 아니라 **성장한 결과 위에 다음 성장이 누적되며 가속되는 구조**입니다. 단계가 진행될수록 블록과 곡선이 함께 커지는 장면으로 표현합니다.

## Composition

- Bottom-left: a small initial rounded block
- Moving right: each successive block becomes noticeably taller and wider
- A smooth accelerating curve runs above or through the blocks
- Small repeated growth nodes show each period building on the previous result
- Optional: a faint straight linear reference line in the background to contrast compound acceleration
- The final block should be clearly larger but not dramatic or financial-market-like

## Final generation prompt

```text
Kitfolio editorial blog cover, 16:9.

Create a clean semi-flat vector infographic showing compound growth as repeated growth building on the previous result. Start with one small rounded block at the lower left, then show a sequence of blocks that become progressively larger as they move to the right. Add a smooth accelerating curve passing through the sequence, with small connected period nodes that make the compounding process visible. A very faint straight reference line may sit in the background to suggest the difference between linear growth and accelerating compound growth. The image should communicate accumulation, time, and future value without looking like a stock-market advertisement.

Use a very light blue-gray background (#F2F5FF), cool blue accents (#2D5DC8 and #6486EF), and neutral blue-gray support tones (#D4D9E5 and #61646F). Use restrained geometric forms, consistent thin outlines, soft rounded corners, subtle diagram nodes, minimal soft shadows, and generous negative space. Front-facing orthographic composition, calm professional modern SaaS mood, low-to-medium visual density, centered and balanced.

No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no percentages, no currency symbols, no coins, no banknotes, no people, no photorealism, no 3D render, no hand-drawn texture, no neon colors, no dark background, no decorative clutter.
```

## Avoid

- A generic upward arrow
- Stock candlesticks
- Coins or investment clichés
- A single straight growth line
- Excessive exponential drama
