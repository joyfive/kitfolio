# Kitfolio Blog Cover Image Prompt

## Series identity

- **Brand:** Kitfolio
- **Use:** Editorial blog cover
- **Format:** 16:9, 1200×675
- **Visual language:** Clean SaaS, semi-flat vector infographic
- **Background:** Very light blue-gray, primarily `#F2F5FF`
- **Primary colors:** `#2D5DC8`, `#6486EF`
- **Supporting colors:** `#D4D9E5`, `#61646F`
- **Optional highlight:** A tiny amount of muted amber, under 5% of the image
- **Composition:** Low-to-medium density, centered visual balance, 15-20% outer negative space
- **Shape language:** Soft rounded rectangles, circles, thin connector lines, subtle nodes
- **Rendering:** Flat or semi-flat, front-facing orthographic view, minimal soft shadow
- **Consistency rule:** Use the same palette, line weight, corner treatment, background tone, and object density across the full nine-image series

## Article

**광고 지표 한 번에 이해하기 | ROAS·CPA·CPC·CPM·CTR**

## Core visual idea

광고 성과를 개별 지표 아이콘으로 나열하지 않고, **노출 → 클릭 → 전환 → 매출**이 하나의 흐름으로 연결되는 퍼널형 데이터 파이프라인으로 표현합니다.

## Composition

- Left: a loose cluster of small exposure nodes
- Center-left: the nodes narrow into a clean funnel
- Center: one interaction node with a subtle ripple indicating a click
- Center-right: a target-like conversion node
- Right: a compact revenue result made of ascending rounded bars or stacked value blocks
- Thin connectors should make all stages feel like one continuous system
- Keep the right-side outcome visually strongest, but do not use currency symbols or numbers

## Final generation prompt

```text
Kitfolio editorial blog cover, 16:9.

Create a clean semi-flat vector infographic showing one continuous advertising performance pipeline from left to right. Begin with a loose cluster of small exposure nodes, guide them through a simple narrowing funnel, continue into a single interaction point with a subtle click ripple, then into a clear conversion target, and finish with compact ascending rounded bars or stacked value blocks representing revenue. The image must communicate impressions, clicks, conversions, and business outcome as connected stages of one system, not as separate icons.

Use a very light blue-gray background (#F2F5FF), cool blue accents (#2D5DC8 and #6486EF), and neutral blue-gray support tones (#D4D9E5 and #61646F). Use restrained geometric forms, consistent thin outlines, soft rounded corners, subtle diagram nodes, minimal soft shadows, and generous negative space. Front-facing orthographic composition, calm professional modern SaaS mood, low-to-medium visual density, centered and balanced.

No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no acronyms, no currency symbols, no logos, no people, no photorealism, no 3D render, no hand-drawn texture, no neon colors, no dark background, no decorative clutter.
```

## Avoid

- Five disconnected metric badges
- A generic marketing megaphone
- Large currency symbols
- Dense dashboard screenshots
- A plain funnel with no visible end-to-end flow
