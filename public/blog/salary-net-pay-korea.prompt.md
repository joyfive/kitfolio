# Kitfolio Blog Cover Image Prompt

## Series identity

- **Brand:** Kitfolio
- **Use:** Editorial blog cover
- **Format:** 16:9, 1200×675
- **Visual language:** Clean SaaS, semi-flat vector infographic
- **Background:** Very light blue-gray, primarily `#F2F5FF`
- **Primary colors:** `#2D5DC8`, `#6486EF`
- **Supporting colors:** `#D4D9E5`, `#61646F`
- **Optional highlight:** A tiny amount of muted amber, under 5% of the image
- **Composition:** Low-to-medium density, centered visual balance, 15-20% outer negative space
- **Shape language:** Soft rounded rectangles, circles, thin connector lines, subtle nodes
- **Rendering:** Flat or semi-flat, front-facing orthographic view, minimal soft shadow
- **Consistency rule:** Use the same palette, line weight, corner treatment, background tone, and object density across the full nine-image series

## Article

**연봉 실수령액은 어떻게 계산될까 | 4대보험과 세금 완전 해부**

## Core visual idea

세전 급여가 하나의 금액 묶음으로 들어와 **공제 항목이 분리되고 최종 실수령액이 남는 구조**를 급여명세서형 도식으로 표현합니다.

## Composition

- Left: one large rounded salary input block or blank payslip panel
- Center: the block passes through a clean deduction splitter
- Four small supporting segments branch downward, representing social insurance deductions
- One separate smaller branch represents tax
- Right: one remaining larger deposit card or wallet-like result block
- The take-home result should remain visually substantial
- No Korean currency symbols, flags, seals, or government iconography

## Final generation prompt

```text
Kitfolio editorial blog cover, 16:9.

Create a clean semi-flat vector infographic showing how gross salary becomes take-home pay after deductions. On the left, place one large rounded salary input block or blank payslip panel. In the center, pass it through a clean splitter. Branch several smaller segments downward to represent social insurance deductions, add one separate smaller branch for tax, and continue the remaining larger portion to the right as a clear take-home deposit card or wallet-like result block. The image should communicate gross pay, deductions, and net pay as a transparent breakdown, not as investment or wealth imagery.

Use a very light blue-gray background (#F2F5FF), cool blue accents (#2D5DC8 and #6486EF), and neutral blue-gray support tones (#D4D9E5 and #61646F). Use restrained geometric forms, consistent thin outlines, soft rounded corners, subtle diagram nodes, minimal soft shadows, and generous negative space. Front-facing orthographic composition, calm professional modern SaaS mood, low-to-medium visual density, centered and balanced. A tiny muted amber accent may be used only on the final take-home result.

No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no percentages, no currency symbols, no national flag, no government seal, no coins, no banknotes, no people, no photorealism, no 3D render, no hand-drawn texture, no neon colors, no dark background, no decorative clutter.
```

## Avoid

- Coin stacks or investment imagery
- A tax office or government building
- Korean won symbols
- A generic wallet illustration with no deduction flow
- Tiny take-home amount that implies most salary disappears
