# Kitfolio Blog Cover Image Prompt

## Series identity

- **Brand:** Kitfolio
- **Use:** Editorial blog cover
- **Format:** 16:9, 1200×675
- **Visual language:** Clean SaaS, semi-flat vector infographic
- **Background:** Very light blue-gray, primarily `#F2F5FF`
- **Primary colors:** `#2D5DC8`, `#6486EF`
- **Supporting colors:** `#D4D9E5`, `#61646F`
- **Optional highlight:** A tiny amount of muted amber, under 5% of the image
- **Composition:** Low-to-medium density, centered visual balance, 15-20% outer negative space
- **Shape language:** Soft rounded rectangles, circles, thin connector lines, subtle nodes
- **Rendering:** Flat or semi-flat, front-facing orthographic view, minimal soft shadow
- **Consistency rule:** Use the same palette, line weight, corner treatment, background tone, and object density across the full nine-image series

## Article

**QR 코드의 원리와 안전하게 만드는 법**

## Core visual idea

QR 코드의 핵심인 **모듈 격자·위치 기준점·콰이어트 존·스캔 동작**을 한 장에 담습니다. 실제 작동 QR이 아니라 QR처럼 보이는 추상 패턴을 사용합니다.

## Composition

- Center-left: a stylized square grid made of rounded modules
- Preserve a generous empty quiet zone around the grid
- Include three prominent corner anchor shapes
- From right to left: a soft scanning beam or frame crosses the grid
- The scan resolves into one small connected destination node or link card
- One or two missing modules may be restored by subtle ghost modules to suggest error correction
- Keep the pattern deliberately non-functional

## Final generation prompt

```text
Kitfolio editorial blog cover, 16:9.

Create a clean semi-flat vector infographic explaining how a QR code stores and reveals information. Place a stylized, deliberately non-functional square grid of rounded modules slightly left of center. Include three prominent corner anchor shapes and preserve a generous empty quiet zone around the grid. Add a soft scanning beam or open scan frame moving across the pattern from the right, then connect the result to one small destination node or link card. Show one or two subtle ghost modules reappearing inside the pattern to suggest error correction. The image should communicate structure, scanning, contrast, quiet space, and reliable recognition.

Use a very light blue-gray background (#F2F5FF), cool blue accents (#2D5DC8 and #6486EF), and neutral blue-gray support tones (#D4D9E5 and #61646F). Use restrained geometric forms, consistent thin outlines, soft rounded corners, subtle diagram nodes, minimal soft shadows, and generous negative space. Front-facing orthographic composition, calm professional modern SaaS mood, low-to-medium visual density, centered and balanced.

No readable text, no letters, no numbers, no URL, no actual scannable QR code, no phone brand, no logos, no people, no photorealism, no 3D render, no hand-drawn texture, no neon colors, no dark background, no decorative clutter.
```

## Avoid

- A functional QR code
- A smartphone mockup as the main subject
- Black-and-white harsh contrast
- Logos embedded in the code
- Overdecorated QR patterns
